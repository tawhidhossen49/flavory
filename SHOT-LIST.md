# Flavory — what to film (4 clips + 2 photo sets)

Your brief says the site uses your own real footage, no AI food and no stock. Everything below can be filmed on a phone.

## Set-up for every clip
- **Backdrop:** matte black (black velvet, black card or a black cloth with no creases), filling the whole frame. It blends into the site's near-black page.
- **Light:** one warm light from the side and slightly behind (window light with the curtain half closed, or a ring light moved to the side). No overhead kitchen light, no flash.
- **Camera:** phone on a tripod, **4K, 30 fps, landscape**. Lock focus and exposure (long-press on the subject). Turn off HDR and stabilisation zoom.
- **Keep the action in the middle third of the frame.** I cut the phone (9:16) version out of the middle, so one landscape take gives me both.
- **One continuous take per clip, 6–10 seconds.** No cuts, no zooming, no panning. The camera either stays still or moves slowly at one speed (slide the phone along a table edge on a book).
- **Nothing written in the frame** — no labels, no screens, no logos except the gold sticker in clip C. The site adds all text.
- **Faces out of frame.** Hands are fine.
- Film **3–4 takes** of each. Send me all of them; I will pick the steadiest.
- Save into `E:\Flavory\raw-video\` with the names below (phone .mp4 or .mov is fine).

### Clip A — "Into the fryer" (hero, top of the home page)
**What happens:** a basket of hand-coated raw pieces is lowered into the oil, it bubbles hard, then the basket lifts out with golden pieces, oil dripping back down.
**Framing:** camera at the height of the fryer rim, side-on, fryer in the centre. Leave the **left third dark and empty** for the headline.
**Length:** 8 s. **Speed:** normal.
**End:** hold for one second on the lifted golden basket.
**Safety:** use a long-handled basket and keep the phone well back from the oil.
Save as `raw-video/hero-fryer.mp4`

### Clip B — "The break" (section 03)
**What happens:** two hands hold one hot crispy strip and pull it apart slowly. Steam rises from the white meat, a few crumbs fall.
**Framing:** close-up, strip in the centre at chest height, black behind.
**Length:** 6 s. **Film in slow motion (120 fps)** if your phone allows; I slow it further.
**End:** both halves apart, steam still rising.
Save as `raw-video/break.mp4`

### Clip C — "Packed hot" (section 06)
**What happens:** looking straight down at an open kraft box. Nuggets are placed in, the lid folds closed, and a finger presses the gold Flavory sticker on top.
**Framing:** phone directly above, box in the exact centre, box filling about half the frame.
**Length:** 8–10 s.
**End:** hold for 1–2 seconds on the closed lid with the sticker dead centre and nothing moving. The site flies through the sticker from this frame.
Save as `raw-video/packed.mp4`

### Clip D — "Coated by hand" (Meet the chef)
**What happens:** Chef Shaheen's hands (no face needed) dip a piece of chicken in egg, press it into the crumbs, turn it, and shake it off.
**Framing:** slightly above, hands and bowls in the centre, apron visible is fine.
**Length:** 8 s. This one loops, so try to end with the hands back where they started.
Save as `raw-video/coating.mp4`
